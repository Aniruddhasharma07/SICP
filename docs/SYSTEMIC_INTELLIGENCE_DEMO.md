## 2. The 5-Step Rapid Walkthrough

Evaluators can click through the interactive **Executive Demonstration Stepper** banner located at the top of the Dossier:

```
[ Step 1: Ingestion ] ➔ [ Step 2: LCA Trace ] ➔ [ Step 3: Sentinel Probe ] ➔ [ Step 4: Heuer AMCH ] ➔ [ Step 5: Gov Action ]
```

### Step 1: Multi-Signal Ingestion & 7-Factor Scoring
- **Action**: Inspect the Ingestion Card and 7-factor breakdown.
- **Narrative**: *"Three separate civic complaints from Ward 44 (Shahpura) and Ward 45 (Gulmohar) arrived within 4 hours reporting complete water outages. The 7-factor deterministic engine scores this at 84% ($S_{\text{sys}} \ge 0.70$), triggering an official Systemic Incident."*

### Step 2: Infrastructure Directed Graph & Upstream LCA
- **Action**: Observe the 5-layer interactive Topology Graph.
- **Narrative**: *"The graph engine traverses the Bhopal Kolar utility network. It traces both failing consumer clusters back to their Lowest Common Ancestor (LCA): Junction J-04 (Shahpura Feeder Hub). Initially, the Upstream Raw Main is suspected (82% support)."*

### Step 3: Proactive Sentinel Inquiry & Branch Differential
- **Action**: Click **"Inject Normal Sentinel Responses (Arera Colony)"** in the Community Sentinel widget.
- **Narrative**: *"Notice that Branch B (Arera Colony) feeds from the same J-04 junction. Instead of waiting, the Sentinel queries 4 citizens in Arera Colony. All 4 report normal water pressure. The Branch Differential Engine instantly detects this asymmetry."*

### Step 4: Richards Heuer AMCH Evidence Matrix Shift
- **Action**: Watch the AMCH Matrix dynamically recalculate.
- **Narrative**: *"Because Branch B is working normally, the Upstream Main Failure hypothesis receives an $E^-$ (Inconsistent) penalty and drops from 82% to 28%. The Local Feeder Failure on Branch A (Shahpura Valve 12) rises to 88% as the leading hypothesis. Falsification criteria explain why."*

### Step 5: Government Authority Sign-Off & Closed-Loop R&D
- **Action**: As a verified Government Officer, click **"Validate Leading Hypothesis"**, then click **"Convert to University R&D Project"**.
- **Narrative**: *"AI and algorithms proposed the hypothesis, but the Human Officer holds final authority. Upon officer validation, the incident closes its loop: an engineering challenge is dispatched to accredited Universities (e.g., IIT Delhi) for hydraulic bypass modeling, and Industry partners for CSR valve co-funding."*

## 3. Resetting the Scenario

To repeat the demonstration at any time:
- Click the **"Reset Scenario"** button in the Executive Stepper or header.
- The state instantaneously reverts to the initial un-probed baseline ($H_1$ leading, sentinel pending).
